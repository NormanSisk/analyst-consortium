Find stocks

A quick, free look that uses no AI and costs nothing. It finds stocks and checks each one in code against the five deal conditions the analysis uses. Tick the ones that interest you and send them to the form below, where the full analysis does the real work. It never places a trade and is not advice.

How to look
Your stocks

Separated by spaces, commas or new lines, up to 300. Stocks outside the US need their exchange ending, such as 0700.HK.

A CSV or text file with one symbol per row, or a column named Symbol or Ticker, such as a broker's export. It is read in this page only and it replaces what is in the box above.

Your filters

Every filter is optional, and a blank one means no filter. Most are answered by Yahoo's free screener; the forward P/E, analyst rating and price filters are applied afterward. Stocks that pass are checked against the same five deal conditions as in the other searches. Open a group below to set filters.
