Run an analysis

Each stock gets its own analysis: analysts gather data, a panel of bulls and bears debates and votes, then a trader, a risk team and a portfolio manager make the call. It never places a trade.

What to analyze

One or more, separated by spaces or commas. Stocks outside the US need their exchange ending, such as 0700.HK. Up to 10 at a time; they run one after another.
